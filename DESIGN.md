# Pelican Bicycle Animation

## Style Prompt

Warm storybook illustration with a breezy coastal morning, using flat vector shapes, friendly rounded contours, hand-drawn motion accents, and a gentle sense of forward momentum. The scene should feel playful and handmade while keeping the pelican and bicycle readable at a glance.

## Colors

- `#F7EFD9` — warm cream canvas
- `#79BFC0` — sea-glass blue for sky and water
- `#F18168` — coral orange for the pelican pouch and highlights
- `#F2B75B` — sunny gold for the beak and small accents
- `#274A57` — deep blue-green ink for outlines and contrast

## Typography

- No visible text; the piece is carried by the illustration and motion.
- If text is added later, use a rounded humanist sans-serif with medium weight.

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use neon cyan, purple gradients, or a dark technology aesthetic.
- Do not use photorealistic textures or detailed feather rendering.
- Do not crowd the frame with labels, interface chrome, or repeated card components.
- Do not use pure black or pure white as the dominant ink or canvas color.
